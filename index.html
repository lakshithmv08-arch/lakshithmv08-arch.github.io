01 / About

Where data, process and people meet

I sit between HR, Operations and Strategy at a large logistics company. My day is about turning headcount, attendance, attrition and payroll data into decisions site and planning teams can act on, and then automating the reporting so nobody has to rebuild it by hand.

Much of what I build is product work in practice: gathering requirements, prototyping, shipping internal tools like an attendance system and an employee self-service portal, and contributing to the BRD for an in-house HRMS. I'm looking for roles in product, product analytics and people analytics where I can keep doing that.
